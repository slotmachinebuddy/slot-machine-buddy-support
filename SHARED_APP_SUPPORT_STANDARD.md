# Shared support standard

User decision, November 7, 2026: use Independent App Support for all apps.

- Contact: independentappsupport@proton.me
- Host: https://slotmachinebuddy.github.io/slot-machine-buddy-support/
- Keep existing app URLs and anchors working.
- Give every app a separate support page and privacy policy describing its actual data practices. Do not copy one app’s policy verbatim to another.
- Link the correct policy and support page from the app and App Store Connect.
- No analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or client-side scripts on this static support hub.
- This is the default for future apps; it does not authorize unrelated changes to existing app releases or accounts.
