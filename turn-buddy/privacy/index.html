Turn Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Turn Buddy has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, third-party software development kits, or developer servers. The developer does not receive your names, timer settings, or session history.

Information on your iPhone

The app saves the current session in local app preferences: the two names you enter, timer durations and remaining time, turn mode, handoff settings, sound and haptic preferences, completion counts, and session state. Appearance is saved separately. Saved sessions restore paused after the app restarts.

Paired Apple Watch

Apple’s WatchConnectivity transfers names, timer settings, current session state, appearance, and session synchronization identifiers and timestamps between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. Watch controls send timer commands back to your phone. The Watch holds received session state in memory. This is communication with your paired device, not a developer server.

Deletion and backups

Ending a session removes the saved session from the iPhone; the appearance preference remains. The cleared state is sent to the paired Watch when connected. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Apple-managed backups may include local session data and preferences according to your Apple account and device settings; Apple controls backup retention and deletion.

Local alerts

Optional sound and haptic alerts are generated on your devices. This version does not send background notifications. It reconciles elapsed time when you return to the app.

Optional support email

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot and improve the app, and prevent abuse. Please include the app name and iOS version; do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Support mail is handled through Proton Mail under its own privacy practices. Correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve the request, maintain support history, meet legal obligations, or prevent abuse. You may request deletion by emailing independentappsupport@proton.me, subject to lawful retention obligations.

This support website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes ordinary web requests under its privacy statement. This site has no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or client-side scripts. Opening a website or composing an email is optional and uses your browser or mail provider.

Policy changes

We will update this policy and the applicable App Store privacy information before distributing changes to the app’s data practices. The effective date identifies this version.
