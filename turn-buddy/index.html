Turn Buddy Support

A shared timer for taking turns or running two repeating countdowns, with optional controls on a paired Apple Watch.

Quick help

  • Set up the session on iPhone. Take turns runs one timer at a time; Two timers runs both independently.
  • With handoff enabled, the next turn waits for your tap.
  • Keep the iPhone and Watch apps open for live updates. Controls pause when the connection is stale.
  • Turn-end notifications are optional and require iOS permission. Background alerts cover up to 60 upcoming finishes within 24 hours; the final repeating-timer alert asks you to reopen to refresh them. Waiting handoff alerts only for the current turn. Focus and iOS settings can silence or delay alerts. Saved sessions restore paused after an app restart.
  • Pause or end the session to stop pending alerts. Closing the app alone does not stop scheduled alerts. End the session to clear saved countdowns; named saved setups and preferences remain. Save up to 20 setups, and remove them individually from Saved setups.
