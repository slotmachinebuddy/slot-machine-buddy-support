Blackjack Study Support

Learn six-deck blackjack with explanations, strategy charts, an untimed ten-question practice quiz, and arithmetic comparing 3:2 and 6:5 natural-blackjack payouts.

For education only. Not for use in a casino or during live gambling. No betting. No money or prizes can be won in this app.

Quick help

  • Use Table rules explained to understand six decks, H17, S17, DAS, and late surrender.
  • New practice follows your selected table rules. Choose mixed, hard-hand, soft-hand or pair practice. Review mistakes retains the original rules for each saved question; a correct retry removes it. Changing current rules begins a new practice session.
  • A quiz score measures answers, not money or predicted gambling returns. Up to 100 missed questions stay locally for review; Clear mistake history removes them.
  • Payout amounts are hypothetical arithmetic examples, not payments or offers.
