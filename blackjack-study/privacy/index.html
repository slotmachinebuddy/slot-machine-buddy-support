Blackjack Study Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Blackjack Study has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party software development kits. The app does not send rule preferences, quiz answers, practice scores, identifiers, diagnostics, location, or financial information to the developer.

Information on your device

The app saves your selected table rules, appearance and practice focus in local app preferences. Starting with version 1.0 build 8, up to 1,000 missed practice questions are saved on this device with their original table rules, your last answer and a miss count. Aggregate correct/attempt counts for regular practice and review are also kept locally by question and table rules, across up to 2,720 supported scenarios. Suggestions use these simple counts; review attempts are separate. There is no event-by-event session log, app account, app-managed device sync or learning-model service. Current session scores and payout examples stay in memory. Earlier versions saved up to 100 mistakes without aggregate progress; upgrading preserves those mistakes but does not reconstruct past scores. There is no developer account or server record of your app use.

The app also stores counts of the actions you choose in regular practice, grouped by question and table rules, to personalize explanations on your device. Choices made before this tracking began are not reconstructed; review-choice frequencies are excluded, and Reset progress deletes these counts while preserving saved mistakes.

Starting with version 1.0 build 20, the app also saves hands you deliberately flag, including their original table rules and an optional answer from the attempt that created the flag, for repeat practice on your device. Flags remain until you unflag them or use Flagged hands → Clear. Correct answers, Clear mistake history and Reset progress do not remove flags. Flags are not sent to the developer, and flagged-hand review does not add regular-practice history.

Deletion and backups

You can change rules and appearance in Settings. A correct answer removes that question from saved mistakes. Use Review mistakes → Clear mistake history to remove the entire notebook. Progress counts remain until you use Practice progress → Reset progress, which removes counts for every rule setting while preserving saved mistakes. These controls do not change your rules or appearance. Deleting the app removes its local data from your iPhone. Apple-managed device backups may contain local preferences according to your device and Apple account settings; backup retention and deletion are controlled through Apple.

If saved data is unreadable, the app preserves an on-device original before recovery. Protected originals may contain items removed from the readable notebook. Clear mistake history or Reset progress removes protected learning originals; Clear flags removes protected flag originals. Settings → Saved data protection → Remove protected originals deletes those copies while keeping current readable data. Copies are not sent to us or synced by the app.

Education only

This app is not for use in a casino or during live gambling. It does not accept bets, provide a bankroll or virtual currency, award money or prizes, track cards, or accept live-hand entry. Its payout examples teach arithmetic and cannot pay or return money.

Optional support email

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot and improve the app, and prevent abuse. Please include the app name and iOS version; do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Support mail is handled through Proton Mail under its own privacy practices. Correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve the request, maintain support history, meet legal obligations, or prevent abuse. You may request deletion by emailing independentappsupport@proton.me, subject to lawful retention obligations.

This support website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes ordinary web requests under its privacy statement. This site has no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or client-side scripts. Opening a website or composing an email is optional and uses your browser or mail provider.

Policy changes

We will update this policy and the applicable App Store privacy information before distributing changes to the app’s data practices. The effective date identifies this version.
