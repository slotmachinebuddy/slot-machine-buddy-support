Privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Slot Machine Buddy does not collect or transmit personal information, usage data, session details, financial information, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data through the app. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party software development kits.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores the planned loss limit, fixed bet, session length, first-player selection, alert preferences, break-reminder progress, skipped turns, and current timer state in app-private storage so an active session can be restored. The Tap When Seen preference remains on the iPhone for future sessions until the player changes it. The app uses fixed labels—Player Left and Player Right—and does not ask for player names.

Device backups managed by Apple may include this local app data according to your Apple account and device settings. Apple controls the backup service and its retention practices.

Deletion and privacy choices

  • Ending a session removes that session’s saved details but keeps the Tap When Seen preference for the next session.
  • The Tap When Seen preference can be changed during setup.
  • Deleting Slot Machine Buddy removes its local app data from the iPhone.
  • Backup retention and deletion are controlled through your Apple account and device settings.

Because the app does not transmit user data, there is no developer-held app account or app-usage record to access, export, correct, or delete. Information you voluntarily send in a support request is handled as described below.

Optional support information

If you post a GitHub issue, the developer receives the information you choose to provide, along with ordinary account information supplied by GitHub. It is used only to respond, troubleshoot the app, improve the app, and protect against abuse.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include casino account details, financial information, precise location, health information, contact information, or other sensitive information.

Support correspondence may be retained while it is needed to resolve the request, maintain an appropriate support history, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate interests. You may edit or delete your own GitHub content subject to GitHub's features and policies. GitHub may retain information under its own policies and legal obligations.

External links

The app includes user-selected links to Apple, this support site, and the National Council on Problem Gambling. Those links open websites outside the app. The destination operator’s privacy practices apply after you leave Slot Machine Buddy.

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web requests under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site itself uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or client-side scripts.

Responsible play and age suitability

Slot Machine Buddy is intended only for adults who are of legal gambling age where they are located.

The app is a pacing timer only. It does not accept wagers, handle money, simulate a casino game, predict results, improve odds, recommend a machine or strategy, prevent losses, or enforce a spending limit. Actual gambling results may differ from the plan.

Use the app only where gambling and mobile-device use are permitted. Follow all laws and casino or venue rules. Never gamble more than you can afford to lose, take regular breaks, and stop if play is no longer enjoyable or affordable.

Find problem gambling help through the National Council on Problem Gambling.
