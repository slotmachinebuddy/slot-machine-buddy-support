Slot Machine Buddy

Privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Slot Machine Buddy does not collect or transmit personal information, usage data, session details, financial information, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data through the app. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party software development kits.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores the pacing amount, fixed bet, session length, first-player selection, alert preferences, hourly-break preference, break-reminder progress, independent player pause state, and current timer state in app-private storage so an active session can be restored. The Tap When Seen and held-player countdown preferences remain on the iPhone for future sessions until the player changes them. The app uses fixed labels—Player Left and Player Right—and does not ask for player names.

Device backups managed by Apple may include this local app data according to your Apple account and device settings. Apple controls the backup service and its retention practices.

Deletion and privacy choices

  • Ending a session removes that session’s saved details but keeps the Tap When Seen, held-player countdown, and hourly-break preferences for the next session.
  • These preferences can be changed during setup, or cleared with Reset Local Data.
  • Deleting Slot Machine Buddy removes its local app data from the iPhone.
  • Backup retention and deletion are controlled through your Apple account and device settings.

Because the app does not transmit user data, there is no developer-held app account or app-usage record to access, export, correct, or delete. Information you voluntarily send in a support request is handled as described below.

Optional support information

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to provide, including your email address. It is used only to respond, troubleshoot the app, improve the app, and protect against abuse.

Support email is handled through Proton Mail. Proton processes messages under its own privacy policy. Do not include casino account details, financial information, precise location, health information, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Support correspondence may be retained while it is needed to resolve the request, maintain an appropriate support history, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate interests. You may ask to have a support request deleted by emailing support, subject to any lawful retention obligations.

External links

The app includes user-selected links to Apple and this support site. The responsible-play section below links to independent help services for each country or region where the app is available. Those links open websites outside the app. The destination operator’s privacy practices apply after you leave Slot Machine Buddy.

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web requests under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site itself uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or client-side scripts.

Responsible play and age suitability

Slot Machine Buddy is intended only for adults who are of legal gambling age where they are located.

The app is a pacing timer only. It does not accept wagers, handle money, simulate a casino game, observe or verify physical spins, track actual spending, predict results, improve odds, recommend a machine or strategy, prevent losses, or enforce a spending limit. Automatic mode shows scheduled turns elapsed and planned turns remaining; it does not detect physical spins. The session always ends when its clock reaches zero, and any pending turn holds are cleared. Actual gambling results may differ from the plan.

Use the app only where gambling and mobile-device use are permitted. Follow all laws and casino or venue rules. Never gamble more than you can afford to lose, take regular breaks, and stop if play is no longer enjoyable or affordable.

Slot Machine Buddy support

Before contacting support, try these quick checks:

  • Use the in-app Test Turn Alert button before starting. Sound and vibration alerts play only while the app is open.
  • If the session appears stopped, check whether one or both player panels are paused. Resume the paused panel when ready.
  • If the fixed bet changes, end the session and create a new plan so the pace matches the new bet.
  • Use Reset Local Data in setup to restore saved alert, hold, and break preferences.
